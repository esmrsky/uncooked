The craft

Built around flat steel and hard edges.

Every panel on a Cybertruck is a mirror. Stretch marks, seams and lifted edges that hide on curvier trucks are obvious here. So every panel is planned before the first cut, film goes on with minimal stretch, and every edge gets checked under raking light.

Know the truck

Flat steel forgives nothing.

  1. 01

    Knife-edge nose

    A hard edge with a full-width light bar. Film is trimmed tight to the light and heat-set so the edge can’t lift at highway speed.

  2. 02

    One unbroken rake

    Hood to roof is a single straight slope. Seams are planned before the first cut so nothing interrupts that line.

  3. 03

    Mirror-flat panels

    Flaws that disappear on curved panels show up here. Film goes on with minimal stretch and gets checked under raking light.

  4. 04

    Door gaps

    Edges are tucked into every gap and relief-cut at the corners, so doors open clean and no bare steel peeks through.

  5. 05

    Hex wheel arches

    The six-sided arches meet black cladding. Straight, even edges here separate a pro install from a weekend job.

  6. 06

    The vault

    The tonneau and sail face the sky. Horizontal panels take the most sun, so film choice and edge sealing matter most up here.

Services

Three ways to change a Cybertruck.

Full color change

Every panel, in any finish from gloss to chrome. The truck looks new, and the stainless underneath stays untouched.

Clear gloss or satin urethane over the stainless. Keeps the factory look and takes the scratches, road grime and fingerprints.

Accents & two-tone

A blacked-out vault, roof, pillars or trim lines. A big change for a smaller commitment.

FAQ

Good questions.

Can film go straight onto the stainless?

Yes. There’s no paint on a Cybertruck, and properly cleaned and decontaminated stainless is an excellent surface for wrap film and PPF. Oils, water spots and embedded iron specks all come off before any film goes on.

Will a wrap damage the truck?

Quality cast film, installed and later removed by a professional within its rated life, comes off factory stainless cleanly. Problems usually come from cheap film, film left on far past its life, or DIY removal.

How long does it take?

A typical full color change is 3–5 days in the shop, including prep, install and a final inspection. Accents and partial wraps are faster. You’ll get a firm schedule with your quote.

What’s the difference between a wrap and PPF?

A color wrap changes the look. Paint protection film is thicker, clear or satin urethane built to absorb scratches and road debris, and most of it heals light marks with heat. Plenty of owners do both.

I’m a few hours away. How does that work?

We plan everything by phone, photos and video first, so you only make the drive twice: to drop off and to pick up.

How do I wash a wrapped Cybertruck?

Hand wash with a pH-neutral soap. Skip brush car washes and keep pressure-washer tips away from film edges. On satin and matte, don’t use wax, polish or anything that adds shine.
